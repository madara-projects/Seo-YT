# Win-Engine OS — Complete User Guide
### *The Creator Intelligence Operating System for YouTube* (v0.13)

Welcome to **Win-Engine OS**! This guide will help you get the most out of your private, local-first YouTube SEO and creator intelligence workstation.

---

## ⚡ Quick Start: 3 Steps to Launch

```
1. Start Docker  ──▶  2. Open Browser  ──▶  3. Connect Channel & Create
   docker compose up    http://localhost:8000   Settings ➔ Creator Studio
```

1. **Start the Engine**: Run `docker compose up -d` in your project folder.
2. **Open Dashboard**: Go to **[http://localhost:8000](http://localhost:8000)** in Chrome or your favorite browser.
3. **Configure Settings**: Open **Settings & APIs** (bottom left), verify your Gemini API key, and connect your YouTube channel.

> [!TIP]
> **Theme Switching**: You can toggle between **Obsidian Premium (Dark Mode)** and **Pearl Light (Clean Light Mode)** at any time using the theme dropdown in the top-right header!

### 2. SEO Creator Studio (The Core Engine)

The 8-stage interactive studio turns your script or raw video idea into an upload-ready metadata package.

```
┌───┐   ┌───┐   ┌───┐   ┌───┐   ┌───┐   ┌───┐   ┌───┐   ┌───┐
│ 1 │──▶│ 2 │──▶│ 3 │──▶│ 4 │──▶│ 5 │──▶│ 6 │──▶│ 7 │──▶│ 8 │
└───┘   └───┘   └───┘   └───┘   └───┘   └───┘   └───┘   └───┘
Idea    Brief   Resrch  Angle   Packag  Compar  Decisn  Check
```

#### The 8 Stages:
1. **Stage 1 / Idea**: Paste your video script, topic, or raw quote.
2. **Stage 2 / Creator Brief**: Refine audience, format (Shorts vs Long-form), viewer promise, and voice-over constraints.
3. **Stage 3 / Research**: Inspect observed public signals, keyword mentions, and competitor velocity.
4. **Stage 4 / Recommended Angle**: Review suggested search vs browse positioning.
5. **Stage 5 / Packaging**: Copy your complete YouTube metadata package:
   - **3 Optimized Title Variants** (Search-Optimized, High-CTR Curiosity, Audience-Specific).
   - **Rich Description** with keywords, chapters, and calls-to-action.
   - **Search Tags & Hashtags** tailored to your format.
   - **Retention & Hook Blueprint**: Pacing guidance, first-5-seconds hook tips, and audio/visual cues.
6. **Stage 6 / Compare**: Compare title and thumbnail options side-by-side.
7. **Stage 7 / Decision**: Confirm your final chosen metadata package.
8. **Stage 8 / Checklist**: Complete manual pre-publish checks (claims accuracy, rights, thumbnail readability).

#### 💡 Example Input for a Quote Short:
```text
Format: YouTube Short (15 seconds, English)

Exact on-screen quote: "The hardest part of healing is accepting that they were never sorry."
Voice-over: None (Ambient melancholy audio)

Target Emotion: Relatable validation and emotional release
```

### 6. Published Audits

Compare what you intended to publish against what actually happened on YouTube.

```
1. GENERATED       ──▶  2. SELECTED      ──▶  3. PUBLISHED     ──▶  4. OBSERVED
   "Build AI Fast"       "Build AI 2026"       "Build AI 2026"      12,450 Views
```

- **4-Tier Flow**: Tracks metadata evolution across all 4 stages:
  1. `GENERATED`: What the AI originally suggested.
  2. `SELECTED`: What you explicitly chose in Creator Studio.
  3. `PUBLISHED`: The live metadata actually published on YouTube.
  4. `OBSERVED`: Real-world 24h, 7d, and 28d view/retention metrics.
- **Comparison Matrix**: Highlights matching fields (`Match`), modified titles (`Changed`), or custom descriptions.

### 7. Experiment Center

Run honest content experiments without mistaking correlation for causation.

```
Experiment: "Question Hooks vs Direct Statements"
Mode: Planned Controlled Experiment
Variable: Hook Structure | Metric: Average View Percentage (AVP)
┌────────────────────┬──────────────┬──────────────┬─────────────┐
│ Group              │ Sample Size  │ Median AVP   │ Difference  │
├────────────────────┼──────────────┼──────────────┼─────────────┤
│ Control (Direct)   │ 6 videos     │ 62.4%        │ Baseline    │
│ Variant (Question) │ 6 videos     │ 71.8%        │ +15.06%     │
└────────────────────┴──────────────┴──────────────┴─────────────┘
```

- **Explicit Group Assignment**: Assign linked published videos to **Control** or **Variant**.
- **Median Comparisons**: Calculates honest metric differentials (AVP, views, engagement rate).
- **Directional Evidence**: Results are reported as directional observations rather than false causal claims.

## 🔒 Safety & Truth Boundaries

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Our Core Guarantees:**
> 1. **Zero Auto-Publishing**: This tool will **never** automatically upload, modify, or delete videos on your YouTube channel. All publishing is done manually by you in YouTube Studio.
> 2. **Read-Only YouTube OAuth**: Permissions are strictly limited to reading channel metadata and analytics.
> 3. **No Fake Numbers**: We never show invented search volumes or promise guaranteed virality. All metrics reflect observed public YouTube data.
> 4. **100% Private & Local**: Your scripts, ideas, and database (`win_engine.db`) stay on your local machine.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

<details>
<summary><strong>Q: Why do my title scores say "Heuristic" instead of guaranteed CTR?</strong></summary>
No algorithm can guarantee human click-through rate. Title scores reflect proven structural best practices (character count, emotional resonance, keyword placement, clarity) to give you the highest probability of success.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Q: What happens if Gemini API quota runs out?</strong></summary>
Win-Engine OS includes an automatic **Local Offline Fallback Engine**. If Gemini is unavailable or quota is exceeded, the tool will instantly generate clean, rule-based metadata packages without crashing.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Q: How do I switch between Dark and Light mode?</strong></summary>
Use the <strong>Theme dropdown</strong> in the top-right header bar. Select <strong>Obsidian Premium</strong> for sleek dark mode or <strong>Pearl Light</strong> for a bright, clean look.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Q: How do I backup my data?</strong></summary>
All your ideas, packages, audits, and settings are stored locally in <code>win_engine.db</code>. You can copy this file anytime or use the automated backup snapshots in the <code>/backups</code> directory.
</details>
