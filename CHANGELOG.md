# Changelog

## 2026-10-10 - AI Shorts understands the quote before it films it, and packages it for search

- The quote is read first: Gemini states its plain meaning, its one emotion, its tone from a fixed list, how viewers search for quotes like it, the hashtag they follow and the emojis that fit. The tone sets the light (night or blue dusk for sad and lonely quotes, sunrise or golden hour for hopeful and healing ones, cold pre-dawn for discipline). A quote whose own words are unmistakable keeps their tone when Gemini drifts ("Discipline is doing it…" is empowering, not hopeful), and Gemini may not add a loss, breakup or event the quote doesn't state. The page shows this reading, the chosen scene and the search phrases under Mood and audio, and keeps the reading even when the scene is thrown away.
- The scene: three real moments a camera could film, built from the quote's situation (never its nouns), and the one a stranger would read from one frame is kept. Symbols that need explaining (clocks, watches, lockets, masks, wrapped or bound objects…), figures of speech, crowds, stillness ("all movement ceases", "stands motionless"), light that changes within an 8-second part, and, for sad, lonely, numb, heartbroken or bittersweet quotes, rooftops, ledges, railings, balconies, bridges and cliff edges are refused or repaired. Scenes used in the last ten AI Shorts are avoided, and the subject, colours, lens and shot size vary instead of "a lone figure in a dark coat" every time.
- The prompts: a shot size and one camera move are always named, the subject is named before "they", a Part 2 adds a new development instead of repeating Part 1, glass gets "no reflection of a face", and contradictions such as "static shot with a slow tracking move" are fixed locally. Length no longer costs a Gemini repair call (up to 180 words). On-screen text never breaks a line on a word like "even" or "don't".
- The package: the exact quote leads the titles when it fits (up to 90 characters), and a title that quietly drops a word of the quote is replaced by the quote. Each title ends with an emoji that fits the quote (🌱 for "My heart finally healed", never 💔 because it says "heart"), and the alternatives don't all share one. Hashtags are #shorts, #quotes and the quote's own theme (#selfworth, #selfrespect, #movingon, #healing, #tamilquotes for Tamil and Tanglish quotes). The tags are 3–5 phrases viewers search for quotes like this one, checked against YouTube's search suggestions (free, no Data API quota), then yt and shorts: "pretend" alone is gone. GREEN needs at least three subject tags, two of them specific to the quote. The description keeps a reflective line that adds no place, person or event the quote doesn't state, and never a stray "yt". A request now makes at most six Gemini calls: the package stage has room for the writer's repair, so a rejected draft is repaired instead of falling back to the local writer, and there are always several title options.
- A tag must belong to the quote: each theme is graded by its tie to the quote (its own words, Gemini's reading of its meaning, its language niche, or its tone's family) and anything else is refused, so a heartbreak quote can no longer carry "football quotes" just because people search it; the tag score follows the grade instead of a fixed 85. A Part 1 that still leaves out what the quote names after its repair (no mother in "My mother's hands…") fails the plan's checks with a clear retry hint instead of passing. AI Shorts has its own documented suggestion limit, `WIN_ENGINE_AI_SHORTS_SUGGEST_MAX_QUERIES` (default 10, 0 turns its check off), and no longer raises the shared limit. The page says a request uses up to six Gemini calls and that the package makes no YouTube Data API research but checks its tags against free search suggestions.
- A tag is judged as a whole phrase: every word in it must come from the quote, Gemini's reading of it, or the vocabulary of emotional and relationship quotes, so "heart surgery quotes" no longer passes on a heartbreak quote because it shares "heart" ("broken heart quotes" and "mother daughter quotes" still do). When Gemini is unavailable, the built-in scenes now include a parent and child, two friends, a couple by the sea, a figure under the stars, storm clouds breaking, a low sun and a mountain path, chosen when the quote names them, and a built-in plan that still misses what the quote names fails its checks instead of passing.
- Love that hurts is read as pain, not romance: a quote where love kills, burns, breaks or haunts ("…only love can kill and keep you alive to feel it") was planned as a warm golden meadow with "romantic quotes" and ❤️. Its tone is now checked against Gemini's own reading of the meaning and corrected to heartbroken (or bittersweet) before the prompt is written, the scene must carry the pain's intensity with a person in it (a peaceful warm landscape triggers the repair), and the package follows: no romantic or happy tags on a painful quote ("love hurts quotes", "heartbreak quotes" instead), #lovehurts or #heartbreak, and a 💔-family emoji. Warm, returned love ("Home is not a place, it's a person") and healed pain ("My heart finally healed…") keep their tone. The package's trace now lists the tags it delivered.
- The Creator page is unchanged: its packages were compared before and after on 48 saved scripts with Gemini off and simulated, field by field and prompt by prompt, and are identical.

## 2026-10-08 - AI Shorts scenes a viewer recognises

- The scene planner now reads the quote first (its plain meaning and who would feel it), then picks one real, relatable moment a viewer understands in one glance (a figure from behind on a pier at dusk, a platform as trains pass), with light that follows the emotion. It no longer asks for "expressive hands" or an "interaction with an object", and never uses symbolic props: "pretend you don't have a heart" was a gloved hand wrapping a pocket watch in wool, and is now a lone figure on a seawall at blue dusk.
- Trimming an over-long prompt keeps its "In the opening…" sentence, which introduces the subject; it was cut first, leaving the later beats about "they" with no one in the scene.

## 2026-10-05 - Package audit: quote titles, honest verdicts, real hashtags

- Quote Short titles: the quote itself, its complete punchline clause, or new words carrying the same idea may lead; a title is refused when it cuts the quote mid-phrase or ends in "…", stops before the quote's turn ("…close enough to need you" without "but never close enough to choose you"), negates or reverses it ("Why I finally stopped loving you" for "I never stopped loving you"), uses a "<keyword> quotes for…" construction, or invents context. "#shorts" is no longer forced into titles (YouTube finds a Short by its format; the description carries it), and an emoji, when used, follows the quote's feeling instead of the footage (no more 🚦 or 🛵 on quotes about honesty).
- Verdicts are honest: unsafe is RED; weak, sparse or quote-only fallback packages are YELLOW; clean and complete ones are GREEN. Brief phrases ("Deep emotional resonance relatable truth and life") can no longer become a GREEN title.
- Hashtags and tags: a quote Short's hashtags are #shorts, #quotes and the quote's feeling (#heartbreak, #selfworth…) instead of phrases coined from tags (#VulnerabilityHangover); the "yt" tag is gone; tags are cleaned (no comma inside a tag, no fragments of the brief, at most 30 characters for a Short and 60 for a long video) and a weak tag is dropped instead of failing or re-generating the whole package. Real searches such as "which iphone to buy" or "obs stream overlay" are kept. On the AI Shorts page, which makes no research, the writer's tags that the quote's own words support ("misunderstood quotes" for a quote about being misunderstood) are kept instead of all being dropped. Copy buttons paste tags as one comma-separated line.
- The backup writer (when Gemini is unavailable) titles the Short with the quote or its punchline, never cut, and writes the exact quote as the description, without template lines.
- Research: 25 results per search (same number of searches); a Short searches Shorts, and a long video searches every length unless its stated length sits well inside YouTube's medium or long band; a quote Short's searches come from the quote, never from the footage notes, while a long video with a quote in it keeps its whole script; the Opportunity Score says it is the same for every option of a run, its keyword-gap count no longer treats phrases competitors share as gaps, the leftover "30 days" rule is gone, and its confidence is LOW under five results. Channel learning only passes a pattern to the writer when the top videos beat the rest by a real margin. Research can no longer use up the Gemini calls the writer needs.
- Options: labels come from what each title does (Shorts carry "Shorts feed"), and every option gets its own readable thumbnail text.
- Backend hardening: impossible ids and limits are refused with 422 instead of 500; request bodies with NaN or Infinity no longer crash the error page; a collector and a manual refresh no longer query the same window twice; History lists AI Shorts runs with a link back to their plan; plans keep the mood hint; Docker drops curl, health-checks with Python, caps the app's memory and CPU, and runs Redis read-only and unprivileged.
- The AI Shorts page tells the creator to put the first line on screen from the very first frame, large and high-contrast, without a typing animation.

## 2026-10-03 - AI Shorts: Google Flow prompts from a quote

- A new "AI Shorts" page beside Creator. The creator types only a quote; Gemini reads its feeling and writes one Google Flow (Veo 3.1) prompt per 8-second part (two parts by default, one to three allowed): Part 1 for Text to Video, Part 2 written for Flow's Extend so it continues the same scene and still stands alone. Every prompt follows Google's Veo structure (camera, subject, action, setting, style, then an ambient-sound line with no dialogue or music), states the vertical 9:16 frame and 8 seconds, keeps the upper-middle of the frame calm for the quote text, and excludes on-screen text, faces, logos and watermarks; the quote's words never appear in a prompt. Deterministic checks patch missing boilerplate, one repair call fixes a leaked quote or a face, and a built-in template library answers when Gemini is unavailable, labelled as a draft.
- The page also gives the Flow steps in Flow's own wording (model menu for aspect ratio 9:16 and length, Add to Scene, Extend, Download), cautions (leave the Veo mark uncovered, tick "Altered or synthetic content" when the scene looks real, mix in your own footage), a plan for which quote lines show in which part, and a lean Shorts package from the existing writer and gate with no YouTube research. Each plan is saved with its package as a History run, so the published Short can be linked and compared later; plans stay on the device (cloud sync carries the run only).
- API: `POST /api/ai-shorts/generate` (rate-limited like other quota-spending requests; at most four Gemini calls and no YouTube calls), `GET /api/ai-shorts/plans`, `GET`/`DELETE /api/ai-shorts/plans/{id}`.
- Database: schema v12 adds the `ai_short_plans` table, for migrated and new databases alike.

## 2026-09-26 - Upload-ready chapters, a Shorts path, quota by bucket and outcome learning

- Chapters: a long video's valid chapters (first at 0:00, at least three, ascending, each at least 10 seconds) are now in the description you copy, in every package option, multilingual package and History copy, placed before the hashtags and never twice. Invalid chapter lists and Shorts get none. A chapter title no longer lends its capital letter to the same word in the prose ("how to Download").
- Shorts: quote Short titles are ranked by how faithfully and clearly they carry the quote's feeling to the viewer, with search phrases only breaking ties, so "Stop explaining yourself to people who refuse to listen" now beats "Alone quotes for when people misunderstand you". Short descriptions are written for viewers (the quote, a reflective line, an optional soft call to action); sentences such as "This video features…", "on-screen text" or "slow motion" are removed or flagged. A Short plans at most three YouTube searches, and its tag score is still reported but no longer decides its verdict or triggers a Gemini repair. A description that carries the exact quote and a short reflection counts as grounded, instead of losing points for the reflection's new words. A quote Short asks Gemini for five distinct angles (the quote's core line in new words, speaking to the viewer, the feeling, a question, one more), and when the gate keeps fewer than three titles the one existing repair call asks for the missing alternatives instead of settling for two. The gate reads "explanation" as a form of "explaining", accepts a title ending "…who you are", and no longer counts "should" or "could" as a topic word. A title unfaithful to the quote can no longer outrank a faithful one, the word after "the quote is-" no longer lends its capital to titles ("Why you should Stop…"), a single-quoted quote with an apostrophe ("you're") is recognised, and the offline fallback drops its emoji rather than cut the creator's quote. Timestamp lines the creator did not supply are removed from every description.
- Quota: every YouTube request is counted per Pacific quota day, per bucket (search calls, other Data API units, Analytics calls) and per key slot or the channel connection, never the key itself. Settings shows today's use against limits you set to match Google Cloud Console (`WIN_ENGINE_YOUTUBE_SEARCH_CALLS_PER_DAY`, default 100; `WIN_ENGINE_YOUTUBE_UNITS_PER_DAY`, default 10,000) and research warns at 90%. Texts that priced a search at 100 units now describe the June 2026 model, where search.list has its own bucket.
- Opportunity Score: each score now shows its five inputs with weights and sources, missing-data warnings and a confidence level, and is labelled a local heuristic, not a prediction of views. A Dashboard card compares past scores with the results of your published videos within comparable groups and says when there is not yet enough evidence; it recommends keeping, recalibrating or retiring the score only with enough videos, and retiring only when the results rule out a useful relationship rather than merely failing to show one.
- Learning: long videos can have up to three packages prepared for YouTube Studio's Test & Compare, with the result you read in Studio recorded against the published video (Shorts are told the test is not available). Completed snapshot windows keep their traffic sources, and cohorts can be compared by dominant traffic source once enough comparable videos exist. A result recorded in Studio stays with the video it was read for: relinking the package asks first, and the result is never moved to a video linked later. Traffic-source groups hold one format and language. A linked video's retention curve can be checked with one YouTube Analytics request (rate-limited like other quota-spending requests); the answer says why when the curve is unavailable. Traffic sources and Studio tests sync with their package; a device on an older version never erases them, and a device that still holds them sends them back.
- Database: schema v11 adds the quota ledger, the Studio tests and the snapshot traffic sources; the live database migrates from v10 with a backup.

## 2026-09-26 - Full audit and hardening

- Numbers are reported only when measured: hidden or missing YouTube statistics, unmeasured scores, unrun analytics and failed syncs show as unavailable instead of zero; lifetime upload views are no longer presented as 28-day views, and a failed part of a channel sync is named.
- Outlier scores need real view, subscriber and date data; unscored rows leave the ranking. Chapters are no longer invented, category presets are no longer shown as keyword signals, model tags are measured against the source, per-title quality labels come from the gate, and the creator's script is never rewritten.
- Research: API keys travel in a header, not the URL; the cache and key rotation are shared across requests; results without statistics are not cached; region and language reach YouTube; channel uploads cost 3 units instead of 102; Tamil and other non-Latin scripts are tokenized correctly; demand research fetches public results only.
- Gemini: capped retry waits, a separate cooldown for research calls, a per-request call and time budget, settings validated at startup, and prompt inputs treated as untrusted.
- YouTube channel: PKCE and a granted-scope check on connect, clear reasons when a connection fails, upstream quota and outage errors mapped to 429/502/503, a revoked grant mapped to 401, snapshot windows collected only once YouTube has reported every day in them, and gone or foreign videos dropped from collection.
- History: relinking a package away from a video with collected evidence now asks for confirmation instead of deleting it silently; a relink without a connected channel keeps verified ownership; performance verdicts compare the same window; failed retries no longer hide data; far fewer database queries per page.
- Cloud sync: rows apply one at a time, moved videos keep their evidence, deletions always apply, endless revision churn and thread death are fixed, no network call holds the database lock, pulls are incremental, and deletions no longer block the request.
- Database: schema v10 keeps the losing local edit with each sync conflict, records which channel each upload belongs to, and stores every format in one spelling; migrations run once at startup before background threads, back up once per process, keep the newest ten backups, and switch to WAL afterwards; a database that fails to prepare reports "degraded" health instead of failing every request, and the collector and cloud sync still start and retry it.
- Learning evidence: every spelling of a format ("Short", "YouTube Shorts", "YouTube Short quote video") is stored and filtered as one value, so Shorts made from the Create page count toward learning; Demand's "Long form" matches every known non-Short format; cohort queries apply every evidence rule; re-running the same script no longer counts its own earlier titles as repetition; Tamil titles are scored by word, not by letter; and a package written in "the video's language" passes the risk filter, refinement and final quality gate like any other.
- Snapshot collection: an outage or unexpected error no longer uses up a window's five attempts; a window is planned only once YouTube has reported all of it; videos verified for another channel are left alone; the Channel page lists only the connected channel's uploads.
- Quota: a cached search is found whichever query led the earlier run; after a failed statistics lookup the next run pays 2 units instead of a new 100-unit search; years and counts are no longer words a result must contain; near-duplicate follow-up searches are skipped; a relink that needs confirmation asks before any YouTube lookup; a script or topic with no words (emoji only) is refused before research. Gemini waits as long as a rate-limited response asks, moves a spent daily quota straight to the backup model, and calls it refused are not counted.
- Generation: one rule decides everywhere whether a video is a Short: its chosen format, then a stated length of up to three minutes, then YouTube's own words ("#shorts", "YouTube Short", "reel") or a standalone quote. "Short ribs" or "in short" no longer turns a tutorial into a Short, and a talk about "lessons" no longer gets Shorts tags. Quoted text becomes the mandatory quote only when the creator supplied or labelled it, or the video is a quote Short, never for a tutorial's button label; visual requirements come only from labelled notes ("Background:", "Visuals:", "B-roll:"); research no longer searches stock "quote concepts" that can mean the opposite of the quote; titles, hashtags and meanings written for test examples are gone; and Tamil, Tanglish and Hindi title and description scores that cannot be measured are reported as not measured instead of a fixed 70.
- Generated copy: fallback titles no longer claim "No Scam", "Real Methods That Work" or a fixed year, and the fallback description no longer claims a walkthrough; emoji variation marks and stray joiners no longer end up in tags or suggestions; Hindi sources ground research concepts; creator chapter lists are validated.
- Safety: DEBUG logging never prints OAuth tokens, client secrets or API keys; every spelling of a record id shares one rate-limit budget, on the current and the upgraded FastAPI alike; allowed hosts may carry a port; a malformed Origin is refused rather than failing; closed experiments refuse new assignments, removals and comparisons; deleting a package linked to a video says, before it happens, that the video's collected evidence goes with it; backups are single self-contained files.
- API: Host allow-list, one error envelope with request IDs and security headers on every response, per-route budgets with a stricter one for quota-spending requests, a request-body cap, stricter validation (trimmed text, ISO dates, normalised regions, strict video IDs), POST-only diagnostics, and no access log (the OAuth code travelled in query strings).
- Classic dashboard: correct audit verdicts and markup, escaped output everywhere, no inline event handlers, no external fonts, and full History paging. The older embedded copy at `/dashboard_legacy` was removed; Settings links to the classic dashboard at `/app`. Bulk deletes of more than 100 packages go in batches, the cloud-deletion message says what was actually requested, missing values read "Not available" rather than zero, and a History page that fails to load keeps the pages already shown.
- React interface: the contract changes above, a confirmation step for evidence-deleting relinks, the research-backed tag and pacing cards ported from the removed dashboard, and dead code and dependencies removed. A failed summary shows "Unavailable" instead of empty-library messages, a slow research no longer pulls you back to its page, dialogs return focus to what opened them, and quota costs are stated as they now are (about 3 units for a channel refresh, 1 for the live check).
- Docker: a `.dockerignore` that keeps databases, backups, secrets, tests and docs out of the image; a read-only root filesystem with all capabilities dropped; pinned base images. Dependencies were upgraded to releases without known vulnerabilities.
- Tests are hermetic (no `.env`, no environment keys, no network, a throwaway database) and run with pytest from `requirements-dev.txt`.
